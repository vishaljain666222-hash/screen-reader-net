Delete Your Account & Data

Accessible AI Academy stores your account and app data locally on your own device — we do not operate a server-side database of user accounts. You can delete your data in either of the two ways below.

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Accessible AI Academy
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete My Account
  4. Confirm when prompted

This immediately and permanently removes your account, wishlist, and course-interest data from your device. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't access it, email us and we'll confirm your request:

Email: vishaljain666222@gmail.com

Please include the name and email address you signed up with. Since your data lives only on your own device (not on any server we operate), there is nothing further for us to delete on our end — we'll confirm this back to you by email, typically within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account name, email, and password
  • Your wishlist
  • Your course "Buy Now" and "Notify Me" interest history

What is not affected

Deleting your account does not affect the Accessible Tools (AI Text Summarizer, Talking Calculator), which don't require an account and don't store any personal data to begin with.
