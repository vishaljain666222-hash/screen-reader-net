Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 July 2026 | Last updated: 8 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Accessible Knowledge Hub ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Accessible AI Academy mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with India's Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

In short: The App stores your account, wishlist, and preference data only on your own device. We do not operate a server that stores your personal data, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

1. Who We Are

Accessible Knowledge Hub is the "Data Fiduciary" responsible for the Accessible AI Academy app. If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, contact our Grievance Officer below.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: the name, email address, and a securely hashed password you provide when you sign up. This is stored locally on your device only.
  • Wishlist & course interest data: which courses you save to your wishlist, and which "Buy Now" or "Notify Me" actions you tap, stored locally on your device.
  • App preferences: your chosen text size, dark/light mode, high-contrast setting, and screen-reader hint preference, stored locally on your device.
  • Text you enter into the AI Text Summarizer or Talking Calculator tools is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere, including to us.

We do not collect payment information, as online payments are not yet enabled in the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Let you log in and keep your session active on your device
  • Remember your wishlist and preferences between app sessions
  • Understand aggregate interest in courses, to help us decide what to build next (this is stored only on your device — we do not currently receive this data on any server)

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

All personal data described above is stored locally on your device using standard Android app storage. We do not currently operate a cloud backend or database, so this data does not leave your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example, via the Share button on a course).

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The App uses your device's own browser and email apps (for links you choose to open) and does not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs at this time.

6. Your Rights

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (visible directly in your Profile within the App)
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Erase your account and associated data at any time
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account, which stops all further local processing
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer if you believe your data has been misused

7. How to Delete Your Account & Data

You can delete your account and all associated local data in two ways:

  • In the App: go to Profile → Delete My Account and confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your account, wishlist, and course-interest data from your device.
  • Without the App: visit our Account & Data Deletion Request page and follow the instructions there, or email us directly at vishaljain666222@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep your account, or until you uninstall the App, whichever happens first. Deleting your account removes it immediately; we do not retain backup copies since we do not operate a server-side database.

9. Children & Persons Requiring Guardian Consent

Accessible AI Academy's courses are intended for learners aged 18 and above. Where a person with a disability uses the App under the care of a lawful guardian, that guardian may exercise any of the rights described in Section 6 on their behalf, consistent with the DPDP Act's special provisions for persons with disabilities.

10. Security

Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing (SHA-256), not in plain text. As all data stays on-device, it is protected by your device's own operating system security (screen lock, encryption at rest, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's lock screen enabled.
